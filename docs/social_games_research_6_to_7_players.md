# Social Games Research & Expansion Blueprint for PartyPop
**Target Group Size:** 6–7 Players  
**Platform Architecture:** Single-Device / Pass-and-Play Mobile Web (Neo-Brutalist Design System)  
**Vibe & Flavor:** High Energy, Social Deduction, Bluffing, Rapid Table Talk, Bengali & Indian Party Friendly

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Taste Profile Analysis

PartyPop's strongest identity is **frictionless, single-device group euphoria**. Your current games (*The Imposter, Tick-Tick-BOOM, Forehead Pop, Fake Artist, Word Trap, Echo Bluff, Mafia, Resistance, Spyfall, Things You Say*) succeed because they:
1. **Never make players download separate apps or juggle 7 different phone screens**.
2. **Turn the single phone into a physical prop**: a ticking bomb passed hand-to-hand, a shared drawing canvas, a forehead-mounted charades screen with gyro tilt, or a secret envelope handed from player to player.
3. **Minimize downtime**: In 6–7 player games, nothing kills a party faster than getting eliminated in minute 2 and having to sit silently for 25 minutes while others play.
4. **Spark immediate laughter, shouting, and psychological debates**: Accusing friends of lying, analyzing suspicious word choices, and debating cultural quirks (Kolkata street food, college adda, family dynamics).

### Why 6–7 Players is the Golden Group Size
At 3–4 players, social deduction is often mathematically constrained (1 spy among 3 is 33% chance; 1 mistake ends the game). At 10+ players, people start having side conversations.  
**6 to 7 players is the board game industry's undisputed "Sweet Spot"**:
- Allows **two competing teams** (3 vs 3, or 3 vs 4) with heated debates inside each team.
- Allows **asymmetric hidden roles** (e.g. 2 Spies/Imposters vs 4–5 Innocents; or 1 Mastermind + 1 Accomplice).
- Guarantees loud room energy without any single player feeling isolated.

## 2. Top 8 Game Blueprints for 6–7 Players

### Game 1: WAVELENGTH (The Telepathic Dial)
> *"Can you tune into how your friends' brains actually think?"*

#### Core Concept & Mechanics
A rotating dial on a 0–100 spectrum between two opposite extremes (e.g., `Worst Kolkata Snack` $\leftrightarrow$ `God-Tier Kolkata Snack`, `Forgivable Crime` $\leftrightarrow$ `Unforgivable Sin`, `Cold` $\leftrightarrow$ `Hot`).
1. A **Psychic** is shown a secret hidden target zone on the wheel (e.g., at 78% towards "God-Tier").
2. The Psychic gives **exactly ONE clue** that lands in that exact position (e.g., *"Egg Roll with extra green chillies"*).
3. The rest of the team fiercely debates where on the slider that clue belongs, physically turning a big tactile rotary wheel on the phone screen.
4. The opposing team gets to make a counter-guess: *"Is the real target to the LEFT or to the RIGHT of where they placed the needle?"*
5. **The Reveal**: The screen flips open to reveal the target! Points are scored based on how close the needle was.

#### Why It's Built for 6–7 Players
- Divides the room into 2 teams (Team A has 3 players, Team B has 3–4 players).
- **Zero downtime**: While Team A is debating, Team B is salivating over whether to bet Left or Right to steal bonus points.
- Sparks hilarious arguments about subjective opinions: *"Bro, how can you say Telebhaja is an 80 on healthy?!"*

#### Tactile Phone Mechanics
- **Neo-Brutal Rotary Dial**: Touch and drag a bright yellow pointer around a semi-circular spectrum with haptic clicks on each degree of turn.
- **Physical "Cover Shutter"**: The Psychic slides down a retro mechanical shutter on the phone to hide the target before passing it to the team.

#### Desi / Bengali Spectrum Decks
- `Mild Street Food` $\longleftrightarrow$ `Destroys Your Stomach at 2 AM`
- `Overrated Bollywood Movie` $\longleftrightarrow$ `Timeless Masterpiece`
- `Pujo Pandal You Visit Once` $\longleftrightarrow$ `Pandal You Stand in 3-Hour Line For`
- `Polite Excuse` $\longleftrightarrow$ `Pure Kolkata Auto Driver Disrespect`

### Game 2: SECRET HITLER / THE PARLIAMENT (Cabinet Bluffing)
> *"Pass the laws, expose the fascists, and trust nobody who claims they had no choice."*

#### Core Concept & Mechanics
The most celebrated high-tension social deduction game in history, perfectly balanced for 6–7 players:
- **6 Players**: 4 Innocents/Liberals vs 2 Spies (1 Fascist + 1 Secret Chancellor).
- **7 Players**: 4 Innocents/Liberals vs 3 Spies (2 Fascists + 1 Secret Chancellor).

1. **Election Phase**: The rotating President nominates a Chancellor. The entire room votes simultaneously: `JA!` (Yes) or `NEIN!` (No).
2. **Legislative Session (Pass-and-Play)**:
   - President draws 3 secret Policy cards from the digital deck (e.g. 2 Red "Coup" policies, 1 Blue "Democratic" policy).
   - President secretly discards 1 card and passes the phone to the Chancellor.
   - Chancellor sees 2 cards, discards 1, and enacts the final policy face-up for the whole room!
3. **The Drama**: A Red policy is enacted! The Chancellor yells: *"He gave me two Red cards! I had no choice!"* The President yells: *"Liar! I gave you one Blue and one Red, you chose the Red one!"* The entire room descends into wild paranoia.

#### Why It's Built for 6–7 Players
- At 6–7 players, the "Hitler / Chancellor" role does not know who their fellow fascists are at the start, forcing them to play cautiously while liberals try to build voting majorities.

#### Phone Architecture
- **Private Ballot Box**: Phone passed between President and Chancellor with private swipe-to-discard cards.
- **Tabletop Display Mode**: Place phone face-up on table during debate showing the active Policy Track and election tracker with ominous sound effects.

### Game 3: ONE NIGHT ULTIMATE WEREWOLF (5-Minute Blitz Deduction)
> *"Zero player elimination. Everyone acts in a 60-second night phase. 5 minutes of total chaos."*

#### Core Concept & Mechanics
In standard Mafia, when you get killed, you sit doing nothing for 30 minutes. In One Night Werewolf:
1. There is **only ONE night phase** and **only ONE vote**. A complete game takes 5 to 7 minutes!
2. Roles have wild action abilities:
   - **Troublemaker**: Secretly swaps the cards of two other players without looking at them.
   - **Robber**: Steals another player's role card and looks at their new role.
   - **Seer**: Looks at one other player's card, or two cards from the unclaimed center deck.
   - **Tanner**: Hates their life and **only wins if they get executed**!
3. When everyone wakes up, nobody is 100% sure who they actually are anymore! Did the Troublemaker make you a Werewolf while you were sleeping?

#### Why It's Built for 6–7 Players
- 6–7 players gives enough role variety (2 Werewolves, 1 Seer, 1 Robber, 1 Troublemaker, 1 Villager, 1 Tanner) for maximum misdirection and cognitive dissonance.
- Fast rounds mean you can play 4–5 rounds in 25 minutes, with scores compounding.

#### Tactile Phone Mechanics
- **Automated Audio Narrator**: The phone speaks with spooky synthesized voice instructions (*"Werewolves, open your eyes and acknowledge each other... Seer, wake up..."*).
- Single device pass-and-tap or phone-in-the-center night taps.

### Game 4: JUST ONE (Cooperative Word Deduction & Duplicate Annihilation)
> *"If you and your friend think of the same clever clue, both clues are ERASED!"*

#### Core Concept & Mechanics
A 100% cooperative party game that has won the prestigious *Spiel des Jahres* (Game of the Year):
1. 1 player is the **Active Guesser** (looks away from the phone).
2. The phone reveals a secret mystery word (e.g. `Biryani`).
3. The phone is passed to each of the other 5–6 players in turn. Each player types **EXACTLY ONE 1-WORD CLUE** (e.g. Player 1 writes `Aloo`, Player 2 writes `Handi`, Player 3 writes `Aloo`...).
4. **The Annihilation Phase**: Before the Guesser turns around, the app checks all typed clues. **Any duplicate clues are instantly deleted and vaporized!**
5. If two players both thought `Aloo` was the smartest clue, both `Aloo`s vanish into thin air!
6. The Guesser opens their eyes and only sees the surviving unique clues. Can they guess the secret word from the few obscure clues that survived?

#### Why It's Built for 6–7 Players
- With 6 clue-givers, the probability of duplicate clashes is delightfully high! It creates high-stakes psychological bluffing:
  - *"Everyone will write 'Spicy', so I shouldn't write that... but what if they think the same thing?!"*

#### Scoring & Competition
- High-score challenge: 13 mystery cards in a pack. How many can your group solve as a collective unit?

### Game 5: MONIKERS / FISHBOWL (The 3-Round Escalating Party Engine)
> *"Round 1: Anything goes. Round 2: One word only. Round 3: Pure silent mime."*

#### Core Concept & Mechanics
Celebrated as one of the funniest party games ever created.
- 6–7 players split into 2 teams.
- A curated deck of 25 hilarious cards (celebrities, weird habits, cultural figures, absurd objects) is generated.

- **Round 1 (Describe Anything)**:
  - 45-second timer. The active player describes cards to their team using any words, sentences, or stories, but cannot say the card name. Once the deck is emptied, Team scores are tallied.
- **Round 2 (ONE WORD ONLY)**:
  - The **EXACT SAME 25 CARDS** are reshuffled!
  - Now, the clue-giver can only say **EXACTLY ONE WORD** as their clue, and the team gets only one guess!
  - Because players remember the cards from Round 1, one strategic word triggers the memory instantly.
- **Round 3 (MIME ONLY / CHARADES)**:
  - The **EXACT SAME 25 CARDS** are reshuffled one last time!
  - No words, no sounds, pure physical charades! By now, inside jokes have evolved. A single funny gesture makes the whole team scream the answer in 2 seconds.

#### Why It's Built for 6–7 Players
- The escalating shared history creates unforgettable group memories. Team rivalry gets fiercely competitive.

### Game 6: DECRYPTO (Secret Code Transmitters vs Interceptors)
> *"Can you communicate with your allies in plain sight without the enemy cracking your cipher?"*

#### Core Concept & Mechanics
Two rival teams (3 vs 3, or 3 vs 4) sit across from each other.
- Each team has 4 secret base words known only to their screen:
  - Team A: `1: Tiger`, `2: Kolkata`, `3: Guitar`, `4: Rain`.
- In each round, Team A's transmitter is given a secret 3-digit code (e.g. `2 - 4 - 1`).
- The transmitter gives 3 clues to the room (e.g. *"City"*, *"Monsoon"*, *"Predator"*).
- Team A easily deduces `2 - 4 - 1`.
- **The Interception**: Team B writes down Team A's clues. Over 3–4 rounds, Team B pieces together what word #2 and word #4 are! By round 3, Team B can intercept and guess Team A's secret code before Team A's own teammates do!

#### Why It's Built for 6–7 Players
- It is the ultimate team-vs-team intellectual party game. Both teams are 100% engaged simultaneously on every single turn.

### Game 7: DECEPTION: MURDER IN KOLKATA (Forensic Science & Crime Scene)
> *"One of your friends is the murderer. The forensic scientist knows who it is, but can only speak through crime scene evidence boards."*

#### Core Concept & Mechanics
- **Roles in 6–7 Players**:
  - 1 **Forensic Scientist** (Game Master / silent ally to investigators).
  - 1 **Murderer** (Bluffing amongst the investigators).
  - 1 **Accomplice** (Optional in 7 players: helps the murderer mislead).
  - 4–5 **Investigators**.
- Each player has 4 Clue cards and 4 Means of Murder cards displayed in front of them.
- In secret, the Murderer taps 1 of their own Clue cards (e.g. `Mustard Oil`) and 1 Means card (e.g. `Drowning`).
- The Forensic Scientist **CANNOT SPEAK A WORD**. Instead, they place digital scene pins on investigative tiles:
  - *Location of Crime:* `Kitchen`
  - *State of the Body:* `Suffocated`
  - *Social Relationship:* `Colleagues`
  - *Weather:* `Heavy Rain`
- Investigators (including the disguised Murderer) debate which player's cards match the forensic tiles.

#### Why It's Built for 6–7 Players
- Combines the deductive depth of Clue with the high-stakes deception of Mafia, but with structured physical evidence instead of random accusations.

### Game 8: SKULL (The Pure Psychological Bluff)
> *"No trivia, no words, no reading. Just pure eye contact, poker faces, and gut instinct."*

#### Core Concept & Mechanics
One of the most legendary bar/pub bluffing games in history:
- Every player has 3 "Rose" cards and 1 "Skull" card.
- Players take turns placing a card face-down on their stack.
- At any moment, instead of placing a card, a player can declare a **Bid**:
  - *"I can flip 4 cards without hitting a single Skull!"*
- Other players can pass or outbid them (*"I can flip 5!"*).
- The highest bidder must now flip cards, starting with **all of their own cards first**, and then choosing other players' cards one by one.
- If they reveal only Roses, they score 1 point (2 points wins the game!).
- If they hit a single Skull, they lose one of their cards permanently!

#### Why It's Built for 6–7 Players
- Takes 30 seconds to teach. Playing it on a single phone with pass-to-play or private card placement turns every round into heart-pounding tension.
